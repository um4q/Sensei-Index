WHERE THESE TWO FILES GO
=============================

Both files in this folder need to land directly inside your InstINDEX
installation folder - the same folder that has gui_app.py,
data_access.py, START_INSTINDEX.bat, etc. in it. Overwrite the existing
copies of both when asked.

1. Equipment_Inspection_Tracker.xlsx
   - The actual data: sheets renamed/reorganized, 24 instruments moved
     into the right area, 14 new ones added, dates fixed.

2. series_registry.json
   - The list of series and what to call each one in the sidebar. This
     is what was missing last time - the workbook alone doesn't carry
     the display names ("Mod 100", "CMM", "Drain Tank 1", etc.), this
     file does. Without it, the app still runs fine, it just shows raw
     series numbers instead of names, and doesn't know series 400 (CMM)
     or 500 (SUS Cooler) exist at all.

Replace BOTH. Then start InstINDEX the normal way.
